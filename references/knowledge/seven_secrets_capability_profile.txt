HOTEL CAPABILITY & EXPERIENCE MAP
Seven Secrets by Hanging Gardens — Lombok, Indonesia
Version: Pre-visit (web research) — To be completed on-site 16–20 April 2026
This document defines what Seven Secrets by Hanging Gardens can and cannot realistically 
offer from an experience, personalization, and event perspective. It is intended as internal 
reference data for CanopIA brief generation, calibrating guest intent against feasible, emotionally 
aligned staff instructions.
1. Hotel Atmosphere & Identity
• Boutique beachfront resort on the northwest coast of Lombok, 24 suites only, positioned 
as a tropical beach sanctuary
• Architecture: Indonesian-tropical fusion with contemporary design elements (designer 
Antal Valentin; Roberto Cavalli, Umbrosa, Swarovski accents)
• Strong positioning around privacy, seclusion, intimacy, and natural beauty — white sand 
beach, coconut palms, views of Mount Agung across the Bali Sea
• Service culture emphasised as personal, owner-involved (owners Valentino and Lydia 
mentioned by name in reviews), staff described as genuinely warm and committed
• Seven Stars Award Winner — positions itself as luxury lifestyle resort
• Couples-oriented (9.7/10 couples rating on Booking.com) but accepts families
Experience implication:
• The hotel is well suited for romantic, restorative, celebration, and intimate experiences
• Small scale means higher personalization potential but also fewer options if things are 
unavailable
• Loud, party-driven, or large-group concepts are misaligned with the property identity
▶ TO COMPLETE ON-SITE: Confirm actual guest mix (% couples vs families vs solo). Observe real 
service culture vs website claims. Note GM Thomas and key staff names.

3. Dining Capabilities
L’Angelo Bianco
• Beachside all-day restaurant, open 7am–9pm
• Cuisine: international and Indonesian, focus on fresh local seafood
• Indoor and open-air with ocean breeze; casual-to-refined atmosphere
• Serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner
The Majesty
• Premier dining venue — positioned as Lombok’s top dining destination
• Curated wine list (premium whites and reds, international selection)
• Contemporary, chic setting; open to both guests and walk-ins
Secret Beach Swim-Up Bar
• Poolside bar on the beach
• Cocktails, wines, beers, and tapas served while guests are in the pool
• Morning-to-evening operation
Anywhere, Anytime Dining
• In-room dining is replaced by flexible dining anywhere on the property
• Guests can order from L’Angelo Bianco menu or chef’s specialty dinner menu
• Delivery to room, balcony, beach, or poolside
Personalization possibilities:
• Dietary accommodation (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free confirmed on pre-arrival form)
• Romantic dinner setups (under the stars — confirmed in honeymoon packages)
• Floating breakfast (confirmed in honeymoon packages)
• Indonesian cooking class (mentioned in guest reviews)
• Occasion-aware dining (celebration, anniversary, proposal)
• Table placement and ambiance adaptation
Constraints:
• Limited number of dining venues — less variety than urban luxury hotels
• Remote location may limit ingredient availability (to confirm on-site)

9. Current Pre-Arrival Process (Observed)
Based on the Google Form received via Booking.com message on 16 April 2026:
What they collect:
• Email, reservation name, contact number (mandatory)
• Flight number and expected arrival time
• Pick-up service selection with pricing (airport IDR 650,000 / harbour IDR 250,000)
• Dietary requirements (checkboxes: None, Vegetarian, Gluten-free, Vegan, Others)
• Activities pre-booking with pricing (3 packages + Others)
• Trip purpose (Leisure, Honeymoon, Family, Special occasion, Others)
• Country of residence
What they do NOT collect:
• Why the guest is coming (context, emotional intent, what matters to them)
• How the guest likes to be treated (interaction style, privacy level, service pace)
• Room comfort preferences (temperature, pillows, scent, light sensitivity)
• Any personal context about who is traveling (couple dynamics, children, celebration 
details)
• Food preferences beyond binary dietary restrictions
• What would make this stay feel special vs generic
CanopIA positioning: This hotel has genuine service warmth but zero structured pre-arrival 
intelligence. The Google Form is logistics + upsell. CanopIA would give their already-caring staff 
the context they need to act on their instincts with precision instead of guessing.
10. Summary Capability Frame
Seven Secrets by Hanging Gardens is best suited for:
• Couples seeking romance, intimacy, and beachfront seclusion
• Honeymoons, proposals, anniversaries, and celebration stays
• Guests valuing personal, owner-driven service over brand consistency
• Restorative, nature-immersed stays with optional adventure elements
• Personalization through tone, attention, flexibility, and human warmth
It is not suited for:
• Business or corporate guests seeking urban convenience• Large groups or party-driven events
• Guests expecting extensive cultural programming or nightlife
• Highly structured itinerary-driven travel
