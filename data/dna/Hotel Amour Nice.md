# Hôtel Amour Nice — preparation profile

Status: public-source preparation, checked 5 October 2026. Not operationally confirmed by the hotel. Operator review pending.

## Identity and atmosphere
The official property page describes an individual, art-filled hotel with collected furniture, colourful rooms, a planted patio and a Greek taverna. This suggests a more informal social setting than the Sukhothai historical profile; that comparison is an interpretation. Ask about the guest's desired rhythm and interaction instead of applying a generic quiet-luxury script.

## Published resources and limits
Rooms differ; some have balconies or bathtubs, and the studio has a sitting area. Rooms are described as having no television or telephone. The property lists a rooftop pool and a separate beach venue nearby. At the date checked, the main page says the rooftop has closed for the season. Beach access and services depend on weather and season. Do not promise the rooftop or treat the beach as part of the room rate.

## Composition possibilities — proposals
Known dining spaces and the beach connection may support a relaxed food-and-sea rhythm, if relevant to the guest and available for their dates. The hotel's visual character may prompt an interest-led conversation, without inferring tastes from nationality or age. Any private setup, bespoke attention, dietary adaptation or celebration requires staff confirmation.

## Information still missing
Concierge availability and the responsible reception contact, room preparation permissions, allergy protocols, mobility access, children's arrangements and all discretionary inclusions remain unconfirmed. A spa, babysitter or special event service must not be invented. Operators must choose the handoff role before creating a stay.
