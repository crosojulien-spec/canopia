# Golden Well / U Zlaté Studně — preparation profile

Status: public-source preparation, checked 5 October 2026. Not operationally confirmed by the hotel. Operator review pending.

## Identity
A Prague boutique hotel whose presentation emphasizes historic surroundings, garden and city views, and an intimate stay. Its restaurant and guided discovery offer useful connections between the property and the city. Let guests describe what attracts them; do not substitute an assumed romantic or cultural profile for their actual words.

## Published capabilities
The official Discover Prague offer combines accommodation and breakfast, a tasting menu for two at Terasa U Zlaté studně, private return transfers and a private walking tour. The published package has a minimum-stay condition and availability is checked directly with the hotel. This proves that the hotel publishes these forms of coordination; it does not establish that every component can be split, changed or supplied without charge.

## Composition opportunities — subject to confirmation
Staff could explore a guest's preferred pace and cultural interests before considering a guide, dining or transfer sequence. For a short or tiring visit, the useful adaptation may be fewer commitments and more breathing room. A food-led stay might suggest a restaurant conversation. A new combination must be presented to the concierge or assigned reception contact as a proposal, with permissions and practical constraints stated.

## Unknowns and limits
Confirm departmental responsibilities, guide options/languages, menu adaptations, dietary controls, mobility requirements, transport arrangements, current availability and prices. No spa, pool, kids' club or bespoke event capability is established by these reviewed pages. Missing information is not proof that a service is impossible.
