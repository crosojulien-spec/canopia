# One hundred offline discovery roleplays

Authorisation: Julien requested 100 simulations, with behaviour revised after each group of ten, on 6 October 2026. This workshop implements that request outside the application.

## Evidence boundary

These are manually authored roleplays in the current Codex conversation. The same assistant writes the fictional guests, plays the discovery host, and reviews the exchanges. They are not independent experiments, blind evaluations, actual outputs of the configured Canopia model, or human guest validation. No Canopia API, application route, database, or paid model comparison is used. Scripted conversation counts must not be reported as a model success rate.

There are 98 exchanges starting with the guest's opening after the ordinary welcome, plus two explicitly labelled continuations (078: repetition repair; 080: withdrawal). Every exchange is written through a normal ending or explicit withdrawal. H means the simulated host; G means the fictional guest. The ordinary welcome and consent explanation are assumed already visible before G's opening. Only facts expressed by G or supplied as reservation context may be treated as known. The author can see the scenario; this is not a hidden-persona experiment.

Structural verification counted 100 distinct transcripts, 686 guest messages and 686 host messages. These are counts of authored text, not a success metric. [The report](../../../docs/100%20offline%20discovery%20simulations.md) explains the results. [The consolidated candidate](Discovery%20behavior%20candidate.md) contains the resulting design. [Verification](Verification.json) records the checks and transcript fingerprints.

## Sequential method

Behaviour 00 is a snapshot of the active conversation-v3.1 prompt. Batch 01 uses that baseline as writing guidance, not as an executed model. Each review records specific findings and introduces the next numbered behavioural revision. Revisions are cumulative and supersede conflicting earlier workshop guidance; all current product boundaries remain. Later batches are authored only after the preceding review has been written. Earlier exchanges remain unchanged, including shortcomings.

Batch 10 uses Behaviour 09. The requested revision after test 100 produces Behaviour 10, which is an untested candidate, not a validated final version. Workshop behaviour is not installed in Canopia. Runtime prompts, model settings, databases and application code remain unchanged.

## Editorial criteria

Review purpose and context; useful personal specificity; inclusion and correct attribution; follow-up value and guest effort; breadth appropriate to this stay; factual fidelity, corrections and boundaries; natural wording; and the timing of closure. A concise successful conversation is legitimate. There is no target turn count or mandatory preference checklist. An unknown hidden preference is not automatically a failure: a relevant conversational opening must exist.

Every case includes a short internal handoff possibility and an unresolved point or review finding. Handoff possibilities are proposals for human consideration, never confirmed amenities, offers, availability or purchases. The workshop assumes no hotel capability beyond hospitality and a human team able to review the information; no real hotel's operational readiness is inferred.
