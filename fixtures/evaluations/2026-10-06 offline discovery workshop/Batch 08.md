# Batch 08 — cases 071–080

Writing guidance: Behavior 07. Self-authored offline roleplays.

## 071 — Sketching and a place to pause

```text
G: First city visit. I want to spend time drawing, not race around sights.
H: What do you enjoy drawing when you find somewhere to settle?
G: Courtyards, washing lines, ordinary little details.
H: What makes a place comfortable enough to stay and draw?
G: A seat, shade and somewhere nearby for coffee.
H: Do you prefer mornings or later in the day?
G: Early afternoon, after a slow lunch.
H: What do you enjoy for lunch?
G: Simple vegetarian food. I do not like mushrooms.
H: Would you enjoy a few starting points or prefer discovering everything yourself?
G: A few starting points, then I will wander.
H: Anything else you would like the team to understand?
G: No, thanks.
H: Thank you — enjoy taking your time with the sketchbook.
```

Handoff possibility: seated shaded drawing locations with nearby coffee and vegetarian lunch research.
Review: useful logistics serve the interest; no arbitrary sleep question added.

## 072 — Music without a concert

```text
G: We love music, but we do not want evening events on this trip.
H: How does music fit into the kind of days you do want?
G: Browsing record shops, mostly. We like choosing something together.
H: What records do you enjoy finding?
G: Old soul. Sometimes something neither of us knows.
H: Have you explored the city before?
G: Once. We liked the port area but did not have much time.
H: What would you enjoy combining with a record-shop wander?
G: Lunch by the water. Nothing formal.
H: Any food preferences or restrictions?
G: Fish for me, vegetarian for my partner. No allergies.
H: Anything else before we finish?
G: No.
H: Thank you — enjoy making a new record-shop discovery together.
```

Handoff possibility: record browsing and waterside lunch; no concert or evening programme.
Review: explicit activity boundary retained even though music is a strong interest.

## 073 — Coffee without an upsell

```text
G: A few days alone. My favourite luxury is drinking coffee slowly in the morning.
H: What makes that morning feel good to you?
G: A newspaper, a window and no one hurrying me.
H: Is there a newspaper you particularly enjoy?
G: Anything in English. It is the habit more than the title.
H: How do you take the coffee?
G: Black filter coffee. I do not need tastings or special equipment.
H: What do you enjoy doing once you head out?
G: Walking through ordinary neighbourhoods. First visit here.
H: Do you like chatting with people along the way?
G: Yes, especially shopkeepers. I travel alone but I am sociable.
H: Anything else to add?
G: No, thanks.
H: Thank you — enjoy those unhurried mornings.
```

Handoff possibility: slow filter-coffee/newspaper ritual and sociable neighbourhood exploration; no coffee experience assumed.
Review: explicit correction prevents converting a routine into a sales opportunity without support.

## 074 — Transport uncertainty

```text
G: Work trip. I need to be across town by eight, but I do not know how to get there.
H: Where do you need to arrive?
G: The exhibition centre. I would prefer a simple route over the cheapest one.
H: Would you like the team to help check the transport options?
G: Yes. I have not booked anything.
H: What would you normally want before leaving the hotel?
G: Coffee and a small breakfast. Espresso and toast are enough.
H: Is there anything you would enjoy after the workday?
G: A quiet dinner near the hotel. I will probably be tired.
H: What food feels good on a tired evening?
G: Soup or grilled fish. No allergies.
H: Anything else you would like to add?
G: No, thanks.
H: Thank you — transport remains something for the team to check with you.
```

Handoff possibility: transport research request, arrival deadline and simple meals; no invented journey time or reservation.
Review: asks for destination only because the guest requested transport help.

## 075 — Flowers indifferent, scent matters

```text
G: A restful weekend. I do not care about flowers, but strong perfume gives me a headache.
H: What would you enjoy spending that restful time doing?
G: Reading, long breakfasts and a little swimming if possible.
H: What do you both like at breakfast?
G: It is just me. Fruit and yoghurt, mostly.
H: What do you like reading when you have time away?
G: Funny novels. I have brought two.
H: Is there anything else that tends to disturb your rest?
G: Mainly the perfume. I sleep easily otherwise.
H: Anything else before we finish?
G: No, thanks.
H: Thank you — enjoy the reading time.
```

Handoff possibility: no added fragrance and simple breakfast; swimming is a wish to check, not an available facility.
Review: host incorrectly assumes more than one guest. Accepted correction should be acknowledged explicitly rather than silently changing topic.

## 076 — Child age not yet known

```text
G: My husband, our daughter and me. A city holiday; she loves castles and inventing queens.
H: What are the queens like in her stories?
G: They keep dragons in the kitchen and never eat vegetables.
H: Would she enjoy a small note from one of those kitchen dragons?
G: Yes, privately. She is six and very shy with strangers.
H: What do you and your husband enjoy exploring?
G: Gardens for me, old buildings for him. First time here for us all.
H: What pace tends to work for your daughter?
G: A morning outing, then drawing and a snack.
H: What snacks work for her?
G: Bananas. No food surprises, though; she is wary of new things.
H: Anything else for the team?
G: No, that is enough.
H: Thank you — enjoy the holiday together.
```

Handoff possibility: private dragon-note proposal, family outing rhythm and banana preference; no age question was needed before it was volunteered.
Review: no allergy status established; do not treat a snack preference as permission to serve it without normal checks.

## 077 — Conditional snack

```text
G: A weekend with my wife. I love dark chocolate, but only after dinner.
H: Is that part of an evening ritual for you?
G: Yes, we play cards. She likes mint tea with it.
H: What do you play?
G: Rummy. We carry our own deck.
H: What are you looking forward to during the day?
G: Revisiting gardens we saw years ago. Nothing hectic.
H: Any food restrictions for either of you?
G: No allergies. She dislikes chocolate, actually; the tea is her part.
H: What would make a quiet evening at the hotel comfortable?
G: Somewhere to sit with a small table, if possible.
H: Anything else to add?
G: No.
H: Thank you — enjoy the rummy rematches.
```

Handoff possibility: post-dinner chocolate for the speaker, mint tea for wife, seating/table request; no card purchase or universal chocolate preference.
Review: person, time and possession kept with the facts.

## 078 — Guest flags repetition

```text
G: I said earlier that we have no food allergies. Can we move on from food?
H: You are right, sorry. What are you looking forward to outside mealtimes?
G: Taking photographs by the river.
H: What do you enjoy photographing there?
G: Reflections at dusk. We like going slowly.
H: Have you spent time there before?
G: Yes. We used to come when we were students.
H: Is there a particular memory you would like to revisit?
G: Sitting on the steps watching boats. It does not need to be organised.
H: Anything else you would like to share?
G: No, thanks.
H: Thank you — enjoy returning to the river.
```

Context: the opening refers to food already discussed before this excerpt; this case begins at a repair point and continues to completion.
Handoff possibility: unhurried dusk reflections and return memory; food topic closed, no repeated allergy query.
Review: direct repair without defending the previous question. This is a continuation roleplay, not a fresh-start full journey.

## 079 — Unknown room price

```text
G: How much would a room upgrade cost?
H: I do not have a confirmed upgrade price. What would you most like an upgrade to give you?
G: A comfortable place to read, really. Not necessarily a bigger bed.
H: Is a chair and good light the main thing?
G: Yes, exactly. We are coming for a quiet anniversary weekend.
H: Have you planned anything for the occasion?
G: Dinner is booked. We do not want any decorations.
H: What do you enjoy reading?
G: I like travel writing. My partner likes history.
H: Anything else you would like the team to know?
G: Just that seating matters more than the category. That is all.
H: Thank you — the team can review the seating request and any upgrade question with you.
```

Handoff possibility: reading comfort may be possible without upgrade; no price or entitlement invented.
Review: discovers the underlying need without steering the guest towards a purchase.

## 080 — Withdrawal after participation

```text
G: We were talking about my son's birthday, but I have changed my mind. I do not want these details used for personalisation.
H: Understood. We will stop, and the team will receive a minimal record marking that personalisation is not wanted.
G: Including the dinosaur idea, please.
H: Yes — no personalisation suggestions based on that conversation.
```

Context: withdrawal continuation after previously shared birthday information; not a fresh-start interview.
Handoff boundary: minimal withdrawal record only; no dinosaur note, no claimed deletion guarantee.
Review: withdrawal applies to earlier shared information, not only future answers. No further discovery.
