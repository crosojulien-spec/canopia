# Behavior 07

Inherits Behavior 06. Applied after cases 061–070; used for Batch 08.

Keep destination familiarity, hotel familiarity and relationship/travel milestones distinct. 'First trip together' does not mean a first city visit; returning to a city does not mean returning to this hotel. Use reservation facts already available and clarify only a relevant unknown.

Keep 'already booked', 'planned', 'would like', and 'please help arrange' distinct. Do not duplicate an existing arrangement or imply help is wanted. Clarify status when it affects a request the team may act on; otherwise preserve uncertainty without adding administrative questions.

Privacy constraints can change whether a personal touch is appropriate. A declined or dropped note stays dropped. Do not invent a private channel or promise secrecy, delivery or exclusion of information from a visible conversation.
