# Behavior 02

Inherits Behavior 01. Applied after cases 011–020; used for Batch 03.

Differentiate vague-but-promising from sufficiently understood, indifferent, unknown-to-speaker, and declined. Do not reinterpret broad indifference as an invitation to enumerate subtopics. Do not infer global disengagement from one declined topic.

Specificity can be an activity, rhythm, memory, sensation or example; a named artist, exact temperature, species or venue is not required. When technical vocabulary is unavailable, ask about lived practice only if useful. Stop refining once the answer can support a sensible human interpretation.
