What this demonstrates
CanopIA does not generate generic upsell lists. It listens to what the guest actually said, identifies 
what matters to them, spots implicit blockers (a couple who wants a romantic dinner but has a child), 
and composes experience suggestions using only what the hotel can deliver. Every suggestion above 
comes from a real guest signal matched against the Seven Secrets activity catalogue.
For the hotel, this means higher conversion on ancillary experiences because the suggestions feel 
personal and relevant rather than transactional. For the guest, it means the concierge already 
understands what they care about before they arrive.
Nothing is booked. Nothing is promised. The concierge presents these as curated options in a warm, 
opt-in conversation. The guest chooses.
